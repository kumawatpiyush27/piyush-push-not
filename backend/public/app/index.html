How segments work

Nothing to set up: segments are recalculated from live data every time you open this page or the campaign creator.

  • A subscriber can be in several segments at once; the same person is never sent one campaign twice.
  • Only subscribers whose notifications are still on are counted, so the reach you see is the reach you get.
  • "Added to cart" and "Bought recently" need Retner running on your storefront (turned on in the theme editor).
